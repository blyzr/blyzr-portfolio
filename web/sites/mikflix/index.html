ZERB

MWAKI (Feat. SOFIYA NZAU)

  • Director
  • Producer
  • Editor

2 weeks before Christmas I was called and asked if I’d direct a music video in Kenya. So naturally I packed my briefcase and hopped several flights to get to Africa. Once there I convinced over a 100 people to go into a place called “Hell’s Gate” where we not only shot the video but where I was attacked by baboons. But that wasn’t enough so I convinced everyone to hike 45 mins into the “Devil’s Bedroom”, a gorge created by volcanic eruption. This is MWAKI.

MAJOR LAZER

ZERB FEAT. SOFIYA NZAU (MAJOR LAZER REMIX)

  • Director
  • Producer
  • Editor

I returned to LA from Nairobi, barely unpacked my suitcase when we decided to RUN IT BACK. So I went back to Kenya for the second time to Direct the Mwaki remix video starring Diplo. Our tech scout consisted of running through traffic and hopping on a MATATU while it was motion and trying to plan while Sean Paul bangers blared through the speakers. We convinced the Nairobi Chief of Police to let us film on the highways while Diplo hung out of the door with no harness. This is MWAKI II.
